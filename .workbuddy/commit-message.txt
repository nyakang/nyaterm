feat(serial): follow the device in titles and move the ask into the dropdowns

Four refinements on top of the connect-time serial prompt.

Editor dropdowns instead of switches: the two independent checkboxes are gone.
The port dropdown now leads with an "ask at connect" entry (selected state
drives `serial_prompt_port`, picking a device clears it and keeps the saved
device as the prompt's default), and the baud rate popover gains an "ask at
connect" row styled like a preset with a check mark. Deferred fields drop their
required asterisk, and in-use devices stay listed but disabled. Batch editing
keeps the plain option list.

A custom device in the connect prompt: the dialog's device dropdown ends with a
"custom" entry that reveals the free-text field (always shown when nothing is
enumerated); picking a listed device hides it again. The saved device stays in
the field behind "custom" so it remains the starting point to edit.

Titles follow the device: a serial connection is titled after the device and
rate it holds (`COM7 @ 115200`, the same shape a quick serial link gets), and
retitle when either changes. A name only counts as the device's while it is
empty, the bare "Serial" a device-less save leaves behind, or still restates the
saved device and rate - anything the user typed is never overwritten, including
on saved connections, so the list and the tab cannot disagree. Switching kind
drops the auto title on new drafts instead of carrying it into SSH.

Tabs are named after what actually opened: the session title is derived from the
port and rate the start really used - the answer to the connect prompt when it
asked - and flows into SerialSessionConfig and the pending start together, so
the placeholder tab, the connecting status, and the finished tab all agree. A
board that came back as COM9 no longer sits in a tab titled COM7.

Tests: dropdown/prompt option selection, custom-port round trip, title
derivation and its "user's own name wins" rule at model, editor, and session
level (including the device-less "Serial" fallback). Locales carry the new
"ask at connect", custom-port, and in-use device strings in all six languages.
